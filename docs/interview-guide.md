# Week 1: owner interview guide

Goal from slide 2: confirm that small food businesses receive paper/PDF invoices and do not track
supplier price changes. Target 5–7 short interviews (10–15 minutes) and 10–15 anonymised sample invoices.

## Who

Owners or buyers of cafés, coffee shops, bakeries, corner stores with 1–3 locations.

## Questions

1. How many supplier invoices do you receive in a week? Paper, PDF, photo in a messenger, e-invoice (ESF)?
2. What happens to an invoice after the delivery? Who looks at it, where is it stored?
3. Do you write prices down anywhere (notebook, Excel, 1C, MoySklad)? How long does it take?
4. When did you last notice that a supplier raised a price? How did you notice, and how long after?
5. Do you compare prices between suppliers for the same product? How often?
6. Have you found arithmetic mistakes or duplicate invoices? What did you do?
7. Would an email or Telegram message "cream +12 %, supplier B is 8 % cheaper" change what you do?
8. What would stop you from photographing every invoice?
9. Can you share 2–3 invoices with prices (we will remove names and BINs)?

## Success criteria for the proposal

- At least 4 of 6 owners do not track prices systematically, **and**
- at least 4 of 6 would act on an alert.

Anonymise sample invoices before any AI test (blur names, BIN, addresses) and prefer the synthetic set in
`samples/` for the free Gemini tier.
